Our Operational Pipeline

A proven 4-step process from corporate surplus to student deployment.

1

Corporate Intake

We collect surplus devices from partner companies and log every asset into inventory.

2

Certified Data Wipe

Industry-grade data destruction ensures every drive is securely erased and verified.

3

OS Refurbishing

Fresh operating systems, quality checks, and spec upgrades make devices classroom-ready.

4

Student Deployment

Devices are assigned to students and schools, closing the digital divide one unit at a time.
